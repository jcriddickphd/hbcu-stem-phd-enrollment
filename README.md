# HBCU Domestic STEM PhD Enrollment, 2019 to 2025

Data and figures behind the ARN Substack article **"America's Domestic STEM PhD Pipeline Is Shrinking. HBCUs Are the Exception."** by Jaret C. Riddick, PhD.

> For R1 and R2 HBCUs, domestic STEM doctoral enrollment has more than doubled since 2019 while the nation stalled.

Article: (https://jaretcriddickphd.substack.com/p/americas-domestic-stem-phd-pipeline)

## Key result

| Segment | 2019 | 2024 | 2025 | Growth, 2019 to 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| HBCU R1/R2 (14 schools) | 62 | 119 | 133 | +114.5% |
| The 27 NYT universities | 5,205 | 5,710 | 5,419 | +4.1% |
| National total | 18,134 | 19,704 | 18,613 | +2.6% |

First-time, full-time domestic STEM doctoral entrants. Domestic means U.S. citizens and permanent residents.

## Check the numbers

Every figure comes from public federal data. To check them:

1. Download the GSS public use files and IPEDS Completions data (see [`data/README.md`](data/README.md)).
2. Apply the definitions in [`METHODS.md`](METHODS.md): first-time, full-time doctoral entrants; domestic means U.S. citizens and permanent residents; STEM means ISCED-F 2013 broad fields 05 to 08.
3. Compare your results with the CSV files in `data/derived/`.
